About

Before joining UIC, I worked on graph machine learning, including graph structure learning, heterogeneous and multiplex graphs, and graph foundation models. I am now expanding my research toward agentic systems and LLM-based agents, with a focus on making agents more effective, efficient, and capable of using long-term memory.
